## App name

Almari: Family Document Vault

## Short description

Your family's papers, IDs, passwords and bank details, locked in one place.

## Full description

Ghar ke saare kaagaz, ek jagah.

Almari keeps your family's important papers safe, organised and ready when you need them: Aadhaar, PAN, passports, driving licences, property papers, insurance, bank accounts, cards, passwords and emergency contacts.

LOCKED SO THAT EVEN WE CANNOT READ IT
• Everything is encrypted on your phone before it is saved or sent.
• The keys stay with your family's devices and your printed Recovery Kit. Our server only ever holds locked boxes.
• Fingerprint or PIN lock, and sensitive numbers stay hidden until you unlock.

YOU CHOOSE WHERE IT IS KEPT
• On this phone only: no account, nothing ever reaches us. Almari works as an organiser on your phone.
• In the cloud: nothing stays on the phone.
• Both: on your phone and synced, so a lost phone loses nothing.

MADE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Invite family members; shared papers reach everyone, "Only me" items stay yours.
• Add children, grandparents and pets as profiles, no email needed.
• Reminders in the app before passports, licences, insurance and PUC expire.
• A family to-do list with due dates and nudges.

READY WHEN IT MATTERS
• Packs: everything for a passport renewal, a home loan or a hospital visit, gathered as one PDF.
• Photos resized to exactly what government forms ask for.
• A masked Aadhaar copy to share safely.
• An emergency card with blood groups, allergies and who to call.
• Bank IFSC codes fill in the branch by themselves.

Almari is not affiliated with the Government of India, UIDAI, or any bank. Copies kept in Almari are for convenience and are not legally valid IDs; DigiLocker and mParivahan hold the official digital versions. Keep the paper originals safe as always.
